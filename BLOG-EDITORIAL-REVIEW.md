# Blog editorial review — publication blockers

The Blog is a client-requested POC. These pages must remain draft/noindex until the studio approves authorship and a qualified reviewer confirms the health and compliance wording.

## Source baseline

The following official sources support only broad safety framing:

- FDA — Think Before You Ink: Tattoo Safety: https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/think-you-ink-tattoo-safety
- FDA — Tattoos & Permanent Makeup Fact Sheet: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/tattoos-permanent-makeup-fact-sheet
- FDA — Tattoo ink microbial contamination guidance: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/guidance-industry-insanitary-conditions-preparation-packing-and-holding-tattoo-inks-and-risk
- CDC/NIOSH — Good, Clean Art: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/2007-138/default.html
- CDC/NIOSH — Safety and Health for Tattooists and Piercers: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/blogs/2009/tattooists-and-piercers.html

These sources support the reality that breaking the skin creates infection risk, contaminated ink can cause harm, unsterile equipment can transmit infection, and professional infection-control practices matter. They do not automatically validate every specific product, timing, medication, legal or studio-compliance claim currently written in the draft posts.

## Post-by-post blockers

### Tattoo Aftercare Basics

Requires review before publication:

- Exact wrap-duration advice.
- Recommendation of specific products or coconut oil.
- Universal cleaning frequency and exercise restrictions.
- Fixed two-week and four-to-six-week healing timelines.
- Statements classifying symptoms as normal or abnormal.

Production wording should defer to the treating artist’s written aftercare instructions and advise professional medical evaluation for concerning symptoms rather than diagnosing through the website.

### Sanitary Practices You Should Expect

Requires evidence or rewording:

- Claims that Flow Inc Ink uses specific equipment or processes.
- Claims about autoclave availability, testing and jewellery grades at this studio.
- Broad statements implying every listed standard is legally mandatory in the studio’s jurisdiction.
- Claims about HIV/hepatitis transmission should be sourced and framed accurately.

The safety message is valid as an educational direction, but studio-specific compliance claims require operational evidence from Flow Inc Ink.

### Why Sobriety Matters

Requires medical and legal review:

- “Alcohol is a blood thinner” presented as a complete clinical explanation.
- Claims about ink spread, healing speed and infection probability.
- Medication lists and fixed pre-session time windows.
- Legal statements about consent and liability.

The studio can state its policy: it may decline service where a client appears unable to provide informed consent or safely participate. Medical explanations should be sourced and limited.

## Required approvals

Before removing Blog `noindex`:

1. Flow Inc Ink approves the author name and publication dates.
2. Flow Inc Ink confirms every studio-specific hygiene and sobriety policy.
3. A qualified health reviewer checks aftercare and medication wording.
4. South African legal/compliance wording is reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction.
5. References are displayed on each relevant article.
6. The email subscription system is actually connected, consent-aware and tested.

Until then, the Blog remains **POC-supported draft content**, not verified professional guidance.
