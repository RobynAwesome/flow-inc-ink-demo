01 — Company profile

Artistry with
an operating spine.

Flow Inc Ink is a registered South African tattoo and body-piercing company operating from Shop M3A at Boulders Shopping Centre in Midrand. Founded by Koketso “Snow” Makhudu, the studio combines professional body art, retail services, tattoo tours, appearances and creative collaborations.

02 — Vision

A recognised African body-art company.

To become one of the country’s most respected and recognised tattoo and piercing studios, expand across Africa and globally, create youth employment and help set higher standards for quality, service and contemporary tattoo culture.

03 — Mission

Quality work. Exceptional service.

To provide high-quality tattoo and piercing services, keep improving and innovating, respond to client demand, build lasting relationships and grow through a distinctive brand, strategic partnerships and responsible expansion.

04 — Culture roots

South African. Youth-led. In motion.

Flow Inc Ink is grounded in South African art, fashion, music, social connection and personal storytelling. The brand moves through studio practice, events, city tours and creative collaborations across Southern Africa.

05 — Founder & head artist

Koketso
“Snow” Makhudu

Professional tattoo artist, body piercer, entrepreneur and shop owner. Snow began his tattoo journey in 2016, completed an apprenticeship in Pretoria in 2017 and built Flow Inc Ink around precision, creativity, professionalism and an elevated client experience.
